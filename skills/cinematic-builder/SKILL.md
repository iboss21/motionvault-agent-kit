# Cinematic builder

1. Use the MotionVault MCP to search templates by the user's subject, mood, and required engine. Inspect candidate metadata, prompt, and actual asset manifests before selecting. Distinguish generated subject footage, still-image camera motion, CSS, Canvas, GLSL, and Three.js accurately.
2. Establish subject, material, lighting, reserved copy space, typography, and a memorable motion event. Read [the cinematic reference](references/cinematic.md) when authoring the shot sequence.
3. Plan five visible checkpoints at0/25/50/75/100%. Record camera, subject, mask/foreground, text, and transition in each. Separate product angles and material macros require separate media; a zoom of one photograph cannot substitute.
4. Get the selected template's source and asset manifest. Preserve the shared scene timeline in live preview and source. Make native wheel/touch/keyboard scrolling drive the story, with reversible transitions, pause, reduced motion, and responsive composition.
5. Build useful page sections and a functional contact form. An exported offline form must identify its submission setup or honest local-file fallback. Do not claim it sent a message without a confirmed service response.
6. Capture actual decoded frames with fonts ready at all five checkpoints on desktop and phone. Inspect the extracted source outside the development app, navigation, a long title, and the exact exported clip when applicable. Present concrete working output and any remaining dependencies.

If Higgsfield is connected and the request authorizes original media generation, use its actual image/video models. Keep job IDs/provenance and resolve partial failures before retrying. Do not buy credits, publish, or touch unrelated infrastructure without user authorization.
